Borrow a drill on Saturday. Return it by the next one.

Larkspur Tool Library lends drills, ladders, saws and 400 other tools to anyone with a library card. No sign-up fee.

Get a library card
Open Saturdays, 10 to 214 Pine Street, behind the co-op.

How borrowing works

Check out in under a minute

Show your card at the desk. We scan the tool and write down the return date.

The deposit comes back

Tools over $50 carry a $20 deposit. You get it back when the tool is returned.

Cleaned after every loan

A volunteer wipes down and tests each tool before it goes back on the shelf. If a blade is dull, it is sharpened first.

412

tools on the shelves, lent out more than 3,000 times since 2022. Larkspur Hardware, Eastside Credit Union and Pine Street Co-op pay for new stock.
